# ADR-003: Robotic Vacuum Armistice and Curfew

## Context & Problem
Autonomous wheeled vacuum units produce high acoustic disturbance and frequently invade feline territorial rest zones, leading to defensive arched-spine posturing.

## Decision
A strict ceasefire treaty is established between biological occupants and robotic cleaners:
- **Operational Curfew:** Cleaners may only operate between 11:00 AM and 13:00 PM when feline agents are safely atop the highest cat tree tier.
- **Safe Clearance:** A 1.5-meter exclusion perimeter must be respected around resting mats.
- **Debris Cooperation:** Any debris produced during experiments outlined in [ADR-001: Tabletop Gravity Testing Protocol](adr-001-tabletop-gravity-testing-protocol.md) may be harvested by the unit only after formal inspection.
