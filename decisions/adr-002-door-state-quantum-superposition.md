# ADR-002: Door State Quantum Superposition

## Context & Problem
Binary door states (100% open or 100% closed) introduce unacceptable latency during tactical corridor patrols. A fully closed door generates high-frequency vocal alerts, while a fully opened door eliminates the thrill of strategic boundary loitering.

## Decision
Interior doors must maintain a permanent state of quantum superposition:
- **Aperture Angle:** Fixed at exactly 42 degrees.
- **Indecision Allowance:** The feline agent must spend a minimum of 45 seconds standing directly across the threshold before selecting a room.
- **Physical Adaptation:** If a room is inaccessible, volume adapts according to [Pattern: Cardboard Box Volumetric Invariance](../patterns/cardboard-box-volumetric-invariance.md).
